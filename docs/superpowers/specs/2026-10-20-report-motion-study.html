What changed since v6

  • Round caps. The pill is a full-width layer clipped to its two spring edges with clip-path: inset(… round 999px). The ends stay circular at any width; only the middle stretches, like a drop, not like a scaled rectangle.
  • Trailing highlight. A specular highlight sits on top of the pill and lags behind the direction of travel by a few pixels, then catches up.
  • Lift while travelling. The pill rises 4% with a soft drop shadow the moment it starts moving and lands when both edges settle.
  • Container transform. Clicking a pin grows a copy of the panel out of that pin, already showing the new finding. When it reaches the panel it is swapped in without a visible seam. No empty panel in between.
  • Layout-true targets. Edges come from offsetLeft, never from transformed rects, so the pill lands exactly even while a neighbour is being bent toward it.
